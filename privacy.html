The short version

Poisette collects nothing, sends nothing and has no network access.

What Poisette reads

The tilt of your head, from headphones with head tracking, through macOS's Core Motion. macOS asks for Motion & Fitness access the first time you press Start or Calibrate. Each reading is used as it arrives to tell upright from slouched, and then discarded.

Whether a microphone is in use by any app, so it can stay quiet during calls. It reads a single on or off flag from macOS. This needs no microphone permission and hears nothing.

Whether an app is full screen, from the size of the front window. Window contents and titles are never read.

What is stored, and where

Inside the app's own sandbox container on your Mac: your calibration, your settings, and a history of minutes (how long you sat upright and slouched, and how many nudges, hour by hour, for 400 days). Today's score and your week are shared with Poisette's own widget. Deleting the app removes all of it; Settings ▸ History can reset it any time.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Poisette has no network entitlement, which means macOS itself will not let it open a network connection. There is no server, no analytics and no account. A CSV export goes only where you save it.
