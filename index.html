What it does

AirPods Pro, AirPods (3rd generation or later), AirPods Max and Beats Fit Pro know the tilt of your head. Poisette lives in the menu bar, learns how you look when you sit tall, and nudges you when your head has been dropped forward for too long.

  • Learns you in three seconds. Sit tall and calibrate; slouch once more if you like, and it sets its own sensitivity.
  • Smart, not nagging. A grace time, glances at the keyboard ignored, turning to talk ignored, and a pause between nudges.
  • A nudge your way. A soft dim of the screen that lifts as you sit up, a chime, a notification, or just the icon.
  • Quiet when it should be. Headphones out, the Mac locked, a call, a full-screen video, outside your active hours.
  • Your progress. Today's score, a streak, week and month charts, your best and worst hours, a widget.
  • Neck stretch breaks that your headphones follow move by move.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later and headphones with head tracking. Poisette is a reminder to sit well, not a medical device.

Privacy in one paragraph

Poisette has no network access, no account and no tracking. Your head's motion is used as it arrives and never stored; only the minutes you sat upright and slouched are kept, on your Mac. The full privacy policy says the same at more length.
